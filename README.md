## Featured Projects

### 🛒 Online Retail II: Customer Behavior, RFM & Retention Analysis

**Python | Pandas | NumPy | Matplotlib | Seaborn**

Analyzed two years of transactional data to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, customer value, RFM segments, repeat purchasing, and cohort retention.

**Key analysis:**

* Analyzed 1M+ transaction records
* Performed data cleaning and feature engineering
* Conducted customer-level revenue and purchase analysis
* Built RFM customer segments
* Analyzed repeat purchasing and cohort retention
* Identified high-value At-Risk and one-time customer opportunities
* Translated findings into actionable business recommendations

**Key findings:**

* Champions represented approximately 21.85% of customers and contributed approximately 68% of customer-level RFM revenue
* 27.74% of customers made only one purchase
* At-Risk customers represented 621 customers and approximately £1.52M in historical revenue
* 72.26% of customers made more than one purchase
